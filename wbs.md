# Work Breakdown Structure (WBS)

## Project

Digital Health Twin for Personalized Health Monitoring

---

## 1. WBS Overview

The Work Breakdown Structure (WBS) divides the Digital Health Twin project into smaller, manageable work packages and activities.

The project is divided into the following major work areas:

1. Project Planning & Requirements
2. Research & Data Preparation
3. System Architecture & Design
4. Digital Health Twin Development
5. AI/ML Development
6. Backend Development
7. Frontend & Dashboard Development
8. System Integration
9. Testing & Evaluation
10. Documentation & Finalization

The WBS follows a hierarchical structure where each major phase is divided into smaller tasks and deliverables.
